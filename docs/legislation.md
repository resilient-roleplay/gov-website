## 🏛️ Purpose

The legislation tab is designed to provide:

- **Clear and concise law references** for all roleplayers.
- **Guidance for law enforcement officers** in investigations and enforcement.
- **Transparency for citizens** regarding rights, obligations, and penalties.


## 📚 Content Overview

- **Federal and State Laws:** All statutes affecting roleplay scenarios.
- **Penal Codes:** Felony and misdemeanor definitions with detailed charges.
- **Points to Prove:** Clear elements required for each offense.
- **Procedures & Protocols:** Guidance on enforcement, prosecution, and investigation.

> The **Resilient DOJ Legislation Tab** ensures fair and consistent application of law across the community while keeping gameplay immersive and realistic.
