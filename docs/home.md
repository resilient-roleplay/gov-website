The **Resilient Department of Justice (DOJ)** is the premier federal law enforcement and regulatory body within the Resilient Roleplay community. Our mission is to uphold the rule of law, protect the rights of citizens, and ensure justice is administered fairly and consistently.


## ⚖️ Legislation and Penal Code

This Hub is your authoritative resource for all legislation, statutes, regulations, and penal code entries governing criminal and civil conduct within our jurisdiction. It provides clear and accessible information on:

- **Federal and State Legislation** – Detailed summaries of laws, their intent, and statutory authority.
- **Penal Code** – Definitions of offenses, classifications of felonies and misdemeanors, penalties, and associated procedures.
- **Procedures and Enforcement Guidelines** – Operational standards for officers, prosecutorial guidance, and investigative frameworks.
- **Rights and Responsibilities** – Guidance on due process, citizen obligations, and law enforcement accountability.


## 📌 How to Use This Hub

Each entry includes:

- **Description** – Explains the nature of the offense or regulation.
- **Points to Prove** – Key elements needed to establish liability or violation.
- **Penalties** – Sentences, fines, and other consequences.

This ensures transparency and consistency across all levels of enforcement and roleplay scenarios.
