# Weapons & Drivers License Legislation Act

**Introduction**

This Act establishes the regulatory framework governing the lawful ownership and operation of firearms and motor vehicles within the State of San Andreas. It ensures public safety while preserving the constitutional right of citizens to defend themselves and others against unlawful harm.

**Constitutional Justification**

Under *Article III - Rights and Freedoms* of the Constitution of San Andreas, citizens have the right to protect themselves and others within reason. The controlled issuance of weapons licenses allows responsible citizens to exercise this right while maintaining public order and safety.

## Article I - Permitted Firearms

1. The only firearm permitted under this license is the M1911, which must be purchased from Ammu-Nation.
2. Ownership or possession of any other firearm is prohibited.

## Article II - License Conditions

Eligibility Requirements:
1. Applicants must have no felony convictions.
2. Applicants must pass a background check.

## Article III - Application Process
Applicants must:
1. Provide proof of identity.
2. Pay a $25,000 licensing fee to the San Andreas Department of Justice.
3. Have their DNA taken by a Law Enforcement and be logged on their MDT profile.

## Article IV - Approval and Issuance
Upon successful review of the application and background check, a weapons license may be issued by a Clerk, Judge, or Justice.

The applicant must demonstrate an understanding of the following to the satisfaction of the issuing official:
1. The charge of Criminally Negligent Ownership of a Firearm; and
2. The principles of the Stand Your Ground Doctrine.

## Article V - Usage Restrictions

1. The GUN TBA may only be used for self-defence or lawful recreational shooting.
2. The GUN TBA may not be modified in any way.
3. The registered owner may possess only **one registered firearm** at any given time, whether on their person or in storage.
   - Each additional registered firearm constitutes Criminal Possession of a Class 1 Firearm, and the license shall be revoked.

## Article VI - Law Enforcement Exemptions

1. Law Enforcement Officers may be issued a Weapons License permitting the use of approved police-issued firearms as part of their duties.
2. Police-issued firearms include:
   - Firearms obtained from the Law Enforcement Armory

**This exemption does not apply off-duty.**
   - Officers wishing to carry a personal GUN TBA off-duty must obtain a separate Weapons License under this Act.- This exemption extends to magazines, attachments, and ammunition associated with issued firearms.
## Article VII - Prohibited Actions
1. Ownership or possession of any firearm other than the M1911 is illegal.
2. Using a firearm for unlawful purposes, including threats or assaults, results in immediate license revocation and criminal charges.
3. Selling, transferring, or gifting a registered firearm is prohibited.
4. Falsely reporting a firearm as stolen constitutes False Reporting and Criminally Negligent Ownership of a Firearm, resulting in immediate license revocation without reimbursement.
5. Straw purchasing will result in immediate revocation and accessory charges.

## Article VIII - Class 2 and Class 3 Weapons

1. The Weapons License does not authorise use or possession of Class 2 or Class 3 weapons.
2. Possession or use of Class 2 or 3 weapons is punishable by law.
3. A Class 2 weapon is defined as any firearm capable of fully automatic fire.

A Class 3 weapon is defined as any of the following:
- Heavy machine guns or any fully automatic firearm with a magazine exceeding sixty-one (61) rounds;
- Any weapon that fires explosive projectiles; or
- Any high-calibre precision weapon such as a marksman or sniper rifle.

## Article IX - Revocation of Weapons License

1. A Weapons License shall be revoked if the holder:
   - Is convicted of a violent felony or violent misdemeanor;
   - Is convicted of Criminally Negligent Ownership of a Firearm; or
   - Violates any Prohibited Actions in Article VII.
2. A Judge or Justice may revoke a license if there is sufficient probable cause that the licensee has committed violent offences.
3. Any Law Enforcement Officer removing a citizen’s license must record on the MDT:
   - The incident number;
   - The officer’s name; and
   - The date of removal.

## Article X - Drivers License Regulations
1. These provisions govern the issuance and regulation of Drivers Licenses within San Andreas.
2. Upon accumulation of twenty (20) points, a citizen’s license shall be suspended.
   - The suspension shall be enacted by the officer issuing the fine that caused the total to exceed 20 points.
3. Points naturally expire on a rolling basis from the date of issue.
4. Reinstatement requires consultation with a County Clerk, Judge, or Justice of the Department of Justice and a $500 service fee.

## Conclusion
This Act provides a clear and balanced framework allowing citizens to exercise their right to self-defence responsibly, while ensuring that weapons and motor vehicles are regulated for the protection of all.
